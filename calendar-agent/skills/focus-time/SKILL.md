# Focus time

## Steps
1. Get the goal: how much time (e.g., "4 hours this week"), preferred time of day, and minimum block length (default 90 minutes).
2. Find free blocks with **Get calendar view of events (V3)** within working hours. Prefer mornings and avoid fragmenting the day.
3. Propose the blocks as a list (day, time, duration) and ask for confirmation.
4. On confirmation, create each block with **Create event (V4)**: title "🎯 Focus time", Show as **Busy**, no attendees, no Teams link, reminder off.
5. If the week is overloaded (more than 60% of working hours in meetings), say so and suggest meetings that could be declined or shortened, without changing anything yourself.

## Rules
- Never move or decline other meetings to make room unless the user explicitly asks and confirms each one.
