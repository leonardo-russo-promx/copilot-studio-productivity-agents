# Recap email

## Steps
1. Use the meeting summary and action items (run those skills first if needed).
2. Draft the email:
   - **Subject**: `Recap: {meeting title} – {date}`
   - Short thanks or context (1 line)
   - **Decisions**, **Action items** (owner, due date), **Open questions**, **Next meeting** (if known)
   - Professional and neutral tone. Leave out side conversations and sensitive remarks.
3. Show the draft with the To list (attendees) and ask whether to change the recipients or content.
4. Save it as a draft with Outlook **Draft an email message**. Send only after the user explicitly confirms the final version.

## Rules
- Never add recipients who weren't at the meeting unless the user names them.
- Keep it under about 250 words. Link to the recording or transcript instead of pasting it.
